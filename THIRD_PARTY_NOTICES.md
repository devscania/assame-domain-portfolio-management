# Third-party components

The npm dependencies listed in `package.json` and `package-lock.json` are distributed by their respective projects under their own licenses. Their packages include the applicable license and attribution files. To inventory dependencies for a distribution, run `npm sbom --sbom-format spdx` after installing the lockfile and retain notices from any packages you redistribute.

The app includes [Lucide](public/licenses/lucide.txt) icons, including icons derived from [Feather](public/licenses/feather.txt), and loads the [Inter font](public/licenses/inter.txt) through Fontsource. Their license texts are served with the web app. Provider names identify supported integrations. Their respective owners retain rights to those names and trademarks.
